{% blocktrans trimmed %} EcoIQ examines the systems we depend on — energy, transport, aviation, industry, buildings, food and agriculture, water, waste and nature — and works out how they can be improved: less harm, less waste, better technology, and capital directed where it creates the greatest measurable long-term benefit. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The work runs from evidence through to verification: understand the current system, generate the realistic options, compare them honestly, finance what holds up, support the execution, and then measure whether the promised benefit actually happened. {% endblocktrans %}

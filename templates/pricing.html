Frequently asked questions

Everything institutional and government buyers ask before engaging EcoIQ.

Why are there no fixed SaaS plans?▾
EcoIQ deployments differ significantly in data volume, integrations, security, users and strategic scope. A bank pilot and a government deployment cannot be priced responsibly as the same standard subscription.
Can we begin with a smaller engagement?▾
Yes. Most organisations begin with an Enterprise Diagnostic or a controlled 90-Day Pilot.
Are third-party data costs included?▾
Standard EcoIQ platform capabilities are included within the agreed scope. Specialist external datasets and licences may be quoted separately.
Can EcoIQ be deployed in the GCC?▾
Yes. EcoIQ is being designed for Arabic and English deployment, GCC-specific data and regional hosting requirements.
Can EcoIQ be hosted privately?▾
Private cloud, dedicated environment and approved regional hosting options can be scoped for enterprise and government clients.
Does payment affect the EcoIQ Score?▾
No. Commercial relationships must never influence evidence, methodology, scores or analytical conclusions.
